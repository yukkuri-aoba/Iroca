  - The original PyTorch checkpoint (mobile_sam.pt, ViT-T variant) was exported to
    ONNX (opset 17) and split into two graphs:
      * encoder: the image encoder as-is (input: preprocessed 1x3x1024x1024 image)
      * decoder: prompt encoder + mask decoder as a fully static graph
        (fixed single click + padding point; dynamic resize postprocessing removed)
  - No weights were retrained or fine-tuned.
